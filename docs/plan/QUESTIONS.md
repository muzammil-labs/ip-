## Phase 4

- **UI-4.4 `data/protectionRows.ts` is new content, not present in the vetted Phase 0 corpus, needed because the task asks for seven distinct IP rows (patent, trade mark, design, copyright, GI, PPV&FR, trade secret) grouped by strength, and the existing `classifyCategories.ts` only carries one bundled "other IP" row per category.** Patent reuses the existing, already-vetted `CAT[cat].rows.patent` text and citations verbatim; its strength (strong/limited/none) is a new judgment call per category, not itself in the corpus. Trade mark, design and copyright are marked "strong" for every category (brand, pack and label protection do not depend on the classify pathway) with their existing citations (`tm-1999`, `des-2000`, `cr-1957`). GI and PPV&FR are marked "none" for every category with a general explanation (GI is for producer associations tied to a region, not a single manufacturer's product; PPV&FR is for a bred plant variety, not an extract or formulation) and cite the relevant Acts (`gi-1999`, `ppvfr`) generally rather than to a specific clause. Trade secret is marked "limited" with no citation, reusing the "no dedicated trade-secret statute" fact already stated in `data/answers.ts` (q2). None of this reads a citation to say something it does not say; it is new synthesis that a human IP reviewer should check, particularly the GI and PPV&FR "none" calls, before the demo.
